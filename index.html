The Good Barber

Two-Sided FinTech Marketplace · Acquired

The Good Barber began with a simple observation: the barbering industry ran on cash, DMs, and no-shows. Clients had no reliable way to find, book, and pay a barber. Shop owners had no software built for how their business actually works — chairs, walk-ins, commission splits.

The answer was a complete two-sided marketplace: a client app for discovery, real-time booking, messaging, and in-app payment — and a dedicated second app for shop owners to run their chairs, staff, schedules, and revenue. A web platform and backend tied the two sides together.

What Was Built
  • Client iOS app (native Swift) — barber discovery, shop detail pages with maps, service selection, real-time booking, appointment history, in-app messaging, reviews, and payment checkout
  • Business iOS app (native Swift) — a separate app for shop owners: booking management, accept/reject flows, client cards, schedule control, payment details, and revenue tracking
  • Native Android app — full client experience on Android with feature parity
  • PHP backend + web platform — APIs powering both apps, account management, OTP verification, referral system, and a public web presence
Under the Hood
  • Two-sided payment architecture: B2C client checkout and B2B shop-side flows with transaction records
  • Real-time booking with confirm/cancel/pending state machines on both sides of the market
  • OTP phone verification, referral program, push notifications, in-app chat
  • Four coordinated codebases shipped by a founder-led remote team of five engineers
Outcome

The platform shipped to the App Store and Google Play, operated in market, and was successfully acquired. Terms remain confidential under NDA.

Automated Options Trading Platform

Quantitative Systems · 40,172 Lines · Live Brokerage

A self-hosted algorithmic trading system built to answer one question with engineering instead of hope: can a disciplined, risk-first framework trade options systematically? The platform ingests live market data, evaluates 19 independent strategies, prices and selects contracts, sizes positions from risk limits, routes orders, and manages exits — all governed by circuit breakers and validated by a research harness before any strategy is allowed to trade.

Market Data & Broker Integration
  • Charles Schwab Trader API with OAuth2 and automatic token refresh
  • Real-time WebSocket streaming for equity quotes and option marks; Level 2 order-book depth multiplexed onto a single broker socket
  • Full option chains — bid/ask, volume, open interest, and live Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega, IV)
  • 2.76M-row local bar cache with automatic backfill; rate-limit governor (90 req/min) with exponential backoff and failover
  • Data-quality gates: stale-quote rejection, crossed-book rejection, disconnect pause
Strategy Engine — 19 Modules
  • Market structure: break of structure, change of character, fractal swing pivots with confirmed-pivot logic that prevents look-ahead
  • Smart-money concepts: fair value gaps, supply/demand zones, liquidity sweeps, order blocks
  • Volume profile: value area, point of control, high/low-volume nodes; order flow: cumulative delta, book imbalance, absorption
  • Multi-timeframe confluence and market-regime classification (trending, balanced, volatility states)
  • Every signal emits a human-readable explanation of why it fired
Risk Management
  • Position sizing derived from the underlying invalidation level — never from available premium
  • Structural stops: exits trigger on the stock breaking structure, not on premium noise from spread or volatility
  • Circuit breaker with drawdown tracking and consecutive-loss limits; daily loss caps; account-level kill switch
  • Signal cooldowns, clearance checks against opposing levels, earnings-date gating, duplicate-order protection
Control Plane & Operations
  • Flask web dashboard — 15 templates, ~11.7K lines of UI, 168 runtime-configurable parameters
  • Live position monitoring with P&L and equity curves; per-symbol analysis with charts, structure overlays, option chain, and Level 2 book
  • Watching panel showing tracked setups and the binding constraint holding each back; trading-halt banner surfacing circuit-breaker state
  • Discord notifications for entries, exits, breaker events, and subsystem health; shadow mode for validating strategies without trading
Machine Learning & Validation
  • Per-symbol adaptive models with nightly automated retraining; regime-conditioned variants
  • AI confirmation layer that can veto or downsize signals below a confidence threshold
  • Event-driven backtester with explicit cost modeling — spread, decay, commissions — and gap-aware fills
  • Monte Carlo simulation, bootstrap confidence intervals, walk-forward and out-of-sample splits, stress grids
Forward Test

Paper execution on live market data — real signals, real quotes, simulated fills — across 10 sessions: 166 trades, +$3,953 net, 1.56 profit factor, 2.76× payoff ratio, 7 of 10 sessions green.

Statistical caveats stated plainly: t-statistic 1.38 (below the ~2.0 threshold for 95% confidence); bootstrap 95% CI on profit factor spans 0.84–2.71; the single best session produced 69% of net profit. This is a forward-test record, not a live track record, and is not annualized.

Personal Health OS

Health & Biometrics · PWA · Live in Production

A browser-based personal health operating system — one place to run daily wellness protocols that would otherwise live across notes apps, reminders, and memory. Built as a PWA, it works identically on mobile and desktop, persists every session, and adapts each day's schedule to what's actually in stock.

The Four Modules
  • Supplement & compound scheduling — daily protocol with timing, dosing guidance, compatibility rules, fasting requirements, site-rotation logging, and a PIN-locked admin panel with auto-calculated dosing for new entries
  • Skincare — morning and evening routines as step-by-step checkoffs, automatic mask-night detection that swaps the routine on schedule, prescription refill tracking
  • Hair protocol — a ten-product regimen tracker (Minoxidil, ketoconazole shampoo, dermaroller, rosemary oil, supplements and more), each with usage instructions, mechanism explanations, and day-of-week scheduling
  • Health dashboard — live Apple Health integration surfacing blood pressure, heart rate, HRV, SpO2, sleep breakdown, weight, and steps; glucose logging with safety guidance; bloodwork tracking for key markers; daily supplement checkoffs
Engineering Details
  • Inventory-aware scheduling: mark any item out of stock and it disappears from daily protocols automatically until restocked
  • Full session persistence — progress, history, and streaks survive every visit
  • PIN-gated admin surface for protocol modification
  • Hosted on Vercel; installable as a PWA on iOS and desktop
